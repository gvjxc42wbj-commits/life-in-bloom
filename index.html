Welcome to Life in Bloom.

Notice your patterns. Find your rhythm. No perfect nights required.

✦ Stored on this device — use Export backup regularly.

Times are approximated in 30-minute blocks. Unmarked time is unknown, not automatically awake. Your information is not uploaded to a server by this app.

How this tracker works

Month: Tap any day to see its 24-hour sleep timeline. Each filled dot represents one recorded hour of sleep; a half-filled dot represents another 30 minutes.

Daily log: Choose Asleep, Resting / unsure, or Awake, then tap 30-minute blocks. Or use the time-range shortcut to fill multiple blocks at once.

Moon Wheel: Tap a segment of the circle to open that day's detail. Colors reflect estimated hours you recorded, not how well you did.

Important: This is a self-reporting journal, not a medical sleep monitor. Logging time in bed as asleep will overestimate your sleep.

Backups: Entries are saved in your browser or installed web app on this device. They do not automatically sync with your other devices. Export your JSON backup regularly.
