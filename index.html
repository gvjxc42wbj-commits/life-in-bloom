Using Life in Bloom

Home: The Home button at the bottom opens your Life in Bloom main page. Tapping the app name at the top also returns Home.

Bottom navigation: Wellness opens Nutrition. Grocery opens your saved things-to-buy list directly. Dog and Money open their sections.

App menu: The three-line button opens Sleep & Mood, Money in Bloom, Food in Bloom, Skills in Bloom, Idea’s in Bloom, Housework, Dog, and Smoking Log. Dog and Money open the same pages from either menu.

Moon Wheel: Tap a segment to open that day's log. Month at a glance includes dated sleep and mood history, plus comparisons based on the feelings you recorded. The comparisons describe entries from the same 8 AM–8 AM tracking day; they do not establish that sleep caused a feeling.

Weekly mood: See seven tracking days, with one row per hour. Scroll the grid sideways on a small screen, or open a day using the date buttons. Tap an awake hour to record or edit how you felt.

Appointments: Add plans for future dates or make them repeat. Use the appointment's usual calendar date and time. The same saved appointment appears in the Weekly Mood grid and Daily Log for its date and time. Plans do not automatically mark you Awake or add an activity or mood. Open an appointment to edit or remove it.

Daily log: Choose Asleep, Unsure, or Awake, then tap 30-minute blocks. Awake opens your activity choices. Pick Cooking, Piano, Sewing, Math, Language, or add an activity of your own. The time-range shortcut fills several blocks at once.

Hourly feelings: Each awake hour can have a feeling and an optional note. Suggested words are shortcuts; you can write your own. Activity labels show what you recorded doing in that hour. Changing a time block keeps any existing mood observation so you can review it.

Dates: Each tracking day runs from 8 AM to 8 AM the next morning. The after-midnight hours are labeled with their actual date. Weeks begin on Monday.

Sleep totals: Hours come from the time blocks you mark yourself. Partial records can be incomplete. A day with some activities recorded has an unknown sleep total until sleep is added or every block is explicitly marked Awake.

Grocery: Sign in to share one household list, monthly food budgets, and Pantry, Refrigerator, and Freezer inventory. Each person edits their own profile. Measurements are personal; the dietary targets and constraints in the shared form can be read by the household. Shopping checkmarks do not create purchases or change inventory. Meal calculations and actual purchase tracking are a later step.

Idea’s in Bloom: Type a note and choose Save note. Open a saved note to edit it, or remove it when you no longer need it.

Personal backups: Export includes this device’s personal sleep, activities, moods, appointments, earlier device grocery list, and Ideas. Import replaces these personal entries only. Shared household Grocery records stay online and are not replaced by a personal backup. Earlier Sleep in Bloom backups are supported.
