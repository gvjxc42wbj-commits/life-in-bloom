Using Life in Bloom

App menu: The three-line button at the top opens your app menu. Choose Sleep & Mood to return to this section.

Moon Wheel: Tap a segment to open that day's log. Month at a glance includes dated sleep and mood history, plus comparisons based on the feelings you recorded. The comparisons describe entries from the same 8 AM–8 AM tracking day; they do not establish that sleep caused a feeling.

Weekly mood: See seven tracking days, with one row per hour. Scroll the grid sideways on a small screen, or open a day using the date buttons. Tap an awake hour to record or edit how you felt.

Appointments: Add plans for future dates or make them repeat. Use the appointment's usual calendar date and time. Planned appointments appear beside your hourly records; they do not automatically mark you Awake or add an activity or mood. Open an appointment to edit or remove it.

Daily log: Choose Asleep, Unsure, or Awake, then tap 30-minute blocks. Awake opens your activity choices. Pick Cooking, Piano, Sewing, Math, Language, or add an activity of your own. The time-range shortcut fills several blocks at once.

Hourly feelings: Each awake hour can have a feeling and an optional note. Suggested words are shortcuts; you can write your own. Activity labels show what you recorded doing in that hour. Changing a time block keeps any existing mood observation so you can review it.

Dates: Each tracking day runs from 8 AM to 8 AM the next morning. The after-midnight hours are labeled with their actual date. Weeks begin on Monday.

Sleep totals: Hours come from the time blocks you mark yourself. Partial records can be incomplete. A day with some activities recorded has an unknown sleep total until sleep is added or every block is explicitly marked Awake.

Backups: Export includes sleep, activities, hourly moods, custom activity names, and appointments with their repeat settings. Earlier Sleep in Bloom backups can also be imported. Import replaces the current entries, so export first if you want to keep a copy.
